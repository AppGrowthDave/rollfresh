Rollfresh Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · AppGrowth ATX LLC

Rollfresh helps you clean up the photos, videos and contacts on your iPhone or iPad. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain terms. The short version: your photos, videos and contacts are analyzed on your device and are never uploaded. There is no Rollfresh account.

Your photos, videos and contacts

With your permission, Rollfresh reads your photo library and contacts to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos and duplicate contacts. All of this analysis happens on your device. We do not upload, copy or store your photos, videos, contacts or their contents on any server, and we cannot see them.

Photos you delete go to your Recently Deleted album, as with any deletion in iOS. When you compress a video or convert a Live Photo, the new version is saved to your library on your device.

Information sent to third parties

Rollfresh does not run its own servers or analytics. Two services receive limited information:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Superwall (paywalls and in-app purchases) A random app user ID, device details (such as model, iOS version, language, region and app version), subscription and purchase status, how you interact with paywalls, and summary numbers from your scan: how many screenshots, similar photos, large videos and blurry photos were found, how much space they take, and which category you chose to clean first. These are counts and sizes only; no photo, video, file name or contact detail is ever included. To show the subscription screen with your own numbers, process purchases and restores, and measure which version of the screen works better.
Apple Purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. On first launch, the app asks Apple's AdServices framework whether you installed Rollfresh from an Apple Search Ads ad; the answer (for example a campaign ID) stays on your device. Payments, and knowing which ads lead to installs. This does not track you across other apps, and the app does not ask for tracking permission.

Superwall's handling of data is described in Superwall's privacy policy. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Notifications

If you allow notifications and start a free trial, the app schedules a local reminder on your device before the trial ends. It is not sent through any server.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Rollfresh is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can change photo and contacts access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Rollfresh works with limited photo access too.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • To ask about or request deletion of the purchase and usage information held by Superwall, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
